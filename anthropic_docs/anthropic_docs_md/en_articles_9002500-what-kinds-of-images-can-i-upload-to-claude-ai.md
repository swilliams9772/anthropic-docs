# What kinds of images can I upload to Claude.ai?

**Source:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9002500-what-kinds-of-images-can-i-upload-to-claude-ai

We currently support the following image types:

* jpeg
* png
* gif
* webp

Images uploaded on Claude.ai can be up to 30MB, and up to 8000x8000 pixels. We recommend avoiding small or low resolution images where possible. 1000x1000 pixels or larger will provide the best results when working with images on Claude.ai.
