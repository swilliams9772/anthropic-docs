# How do I enable or disable the artifacts feature?

**Source:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9487308-how-do-i-enable-or-disable-the-artifacts-feature

To adjust your artifacts preferences, navigate to your [Capabilities Settings](https://claude.ai/settings/capabilities) by clicking on your initials or name in the lower left corner and selecting Settings > Capabilities. Find "Artifacts" and toggle it on or off.

**Note for Claude for Work users:** Team and Enterprise plan users have access to the full artifacts experience, including the dedicated artifacts space in the sidebar, AI-powered artifacts capabilities, and secure organization-only sharing. Your administrator may manage some settings at the organization level.
