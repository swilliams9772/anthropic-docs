# Choosing a Claude Plan

**Source:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11049762-choosing-a-claude-plan

Use the following guide to determine which plan is right for you.

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan** | **Price** | **Billing Interval** | **Usage Capacity** | **Best For** |
| Free | $0 | N/A | [Limited](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/8602283-does-claude-ai-have-any-message-limits) | Occasional use |
| Pro | $20/month  $200/year | Monthly or annual | [Standard](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/8324991-about-claude-s-pro-plan-usage) | Regular use |
| Max 5x | $100 | Monthly | [5x Pro capacity](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/11014257-about-claude-s-max-plan-usage) per session | Frequent users who work with Claude on a variety of tasks |
| Max 20x | $200 | Monthly | [20x Pro capacity](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/11014257-about-claude-s-max-plan-usage) per session | Daily users who collaborate often with Claude for most tasks |

The plans listed above are intended for individual users. Looking for a plan for your organization or company? Read more about our Team and Enterprise plans:

* [What is the Team plan?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9266767-what-is-the-team-plan)
* [What is the Enterprise plan?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9797531-what-is-the-enterprise-plan)
