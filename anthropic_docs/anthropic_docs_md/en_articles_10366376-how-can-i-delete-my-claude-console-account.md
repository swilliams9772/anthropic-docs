# How can I delete my Claude Console account?

**Source:** https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10366376-how-can-i-delete-my-claude-console-account

Only Console Admins can request deletion. See [Claude Console Roles and Permissions](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10186004-claude-console-roles-and-permissions) for a full overview of your role's capabilities.

We don't offer self-serve deletion for Claude Console organizations at this time, so our Support team will need to process the deletion for you manually.

# Before requesting deletion

* Confirm which specific Console organization needs to be deleted.
* Ensure all users within the organization are notified.
* Back up any necessary data as this action cannot be reversed.

# How to request deletion

1. Log in to your Console Admin account.
2. Click your initials in the lower left corner of your Claude Console account then "Get help," or the message icon in the bottom right of our Help Center, to [contact the team](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9015913-how-to-get-support) and request deletion.
3. Our Support team will verify and process the deletion request.
4. Once processed, the deletion is permanent and affects all organization users.
